9. Boundary / design decisions
- key_focus: smallest highest-conviction set; future high-frequency candidate pool
- focus: broader operational/reporting set
- archive_targets: history accumulation targets split by minute / 15min / daily frequency
- list-level asset_type is kept as multi_asset for the default cross-asset pools
- item-level asset_category is the precise business classification
- owner schema is future-compatible for expansion to customer / account / org even though Phase 1 uses only default/default
- future integration with symbol_universe should remain validation/enrichment oriented, not a blocker for non-stock business objects
